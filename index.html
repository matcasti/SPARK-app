SPARK

Welcome

Reconstruct the vagal-like and sympathetic-like drives of the heart from RR intervals alone. Everything runs in your browser: your data are never uploaded.

Analyse a recording

Drop a .txt or .csv of RR intervals (ms, s or min, auto-detected) or load an example. Optionally clean artifacts first with the running-median / MAD gate used in the data preparation of the R pipeline. Get ν₀, the two time constants, the noise, standard errors and 95% bands.

See the drives

Vagal-like (x_p) and sympathetic-like (x_s) trajectories, the phase map and the state-space landscape. Rest, stress and recovery are detected from heart rate, or you draw them.

Go live with a Polar H10

Switch to Live, connect the strap over Bluetooth (Chrome or Edge) and watch the drives update at every beat. Send the session to the analysis when you finish.

Check, export, reproduce

Diagnostics and HRV statistics (observed vs filtered), a parameter playground, PNG, CSV, JSON and HTML exports, and an R script that reproduces the fit.

For the curious: the model and the engine

The model (M3). Two latent Ornstein–Uhlenbeck drives, x_p (fast, a time constant of seconds, vagal-like) and x_s (slow, minutes, sympathetic-like), set the instantaneous rate ν(t) = ν₀ − x_p + x_s of an integrate-and-fire pacemaker: a beat occurs when the integrated phase reaches 1 and the phase then resets. Noise at the threshold gives the RR jitter.

Estimation. An exact Kalman filter on the phase-domain model, with ν₀ and the global scale profiled out (concentrated GLS likelihood). Three parameters (κ_S, κ_P, Λ_R) are fitted by maximum a posteriori, with weak log-normal priors on τ_P, τ_S and the jitter and a prior on the separation of the two time scales. Standard errors come from the numerical Hessian (delta method).

The spectral anchor. The vagal-to-sympathetic variance ratio V_P/V_S cannot be identified from RR alone. It is fixed from a Morlet wavelet spectrum of heart rate, split logistically at 0.1 Hz with a vagal floor (default 0.25). It decides how a sustained change is shared between the two drives, so read that split as conditional on the anchor.

The engine. A JavaScript port of the R engine (model_engine.R) that agrees with it to about 1e-9 in states and likelihood. Heavy work runs in a Web Worker, and ectopic or noisy beats are down-weighted by an artifact multiplier. The states are model-based latent drives, not recordings of nerve activity; in Live they are causal filtered estimates, so they lag sudden changes.
